# Evidence Integrity Standard

## Purpose

The CYBERNOVA SOC Operations Lab uses synthetic and authorized laboratory
telemetry to demonstrate professional Security Operations Center workflows.

No unauthorized production data, credentials, private information, or
third-party confidential information should be introduced into this repository.

## Evidence Classification

Every investigation should distinguish between the following categories.

### Observed Evidence

Facts directly supported by laboratory telemetry, logs, alerts, files,
timestamps, hashes, or other collected artifacts.

### Analyst Interpretation

A reasoned interpretation of observed evidence.

### Hypothesis

A proposed explanation that has not yet been sufficiently validated.

### Confirmed Finding

A conclusion supported by sufficient evidence and validation.

### Recommended Action

A proposed containment, eradication, recovery, detection, or monitoring action.

### Limitation

Any condition that prevents complete validation or reduces confidence in
the conclusion.

## Evidence Principles

Investigations should:

1. Preserve original evidence where practical.
2. Record timestamps and relevant context.
3. Avoid modifying original artifacts during analysis.
4. Record hashes for relevant files where appropriate.
5. Separate facts from assumptions.
6. Document uncertainty.
7. Avoid overstating conclusions.
8. Clearly identify synthetic laboratory evidence.
9. Maintain reproducibility where practical.
10. Never represent laboratory activity as production employment experience.

## Professional Standard

The objective is not to manufacture an impressive incident.

The objective is to demonstrate how a professional security analyst reasons
from evidence to a defensible conclusion.
